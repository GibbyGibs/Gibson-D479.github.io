Frequently Asked Questions

Important information to know before visiting Taniti.

What currency is used?

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen. Several banks provide currency exchange, and many businesses accept major credit cards.

What type of electrical outlets are used?

Taniti uses 120-volt outlets, the same voltage used in the United States.

What is the drinking age?

The drinking age is 18. Alcohol cannot be served or sold between midnight and 9:00 a.m.

Is English spoken?

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Very little English is spoken in rural areas, particularly by older residents.

What medical services are available?

Taniti has one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has many multilingual employees.

Is Taniti safe?

Violent crime is very rare. However, pickpocketing and other petty crimes have increased as tourism has increased.

Do holidays affect attractions?

Yes. Taniti has many national holidays. Tourist attractions and restaurants may be closed during holidays.
