Transportation

Learn how to arrive and travel around Taniti.

Airport

Most visitors arrive by air at Taniti's small airport.

Public Bus

Public buses serve Taniti City from 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. every day.

Private Bus

Private buses serve the rest of the island.

Taxis

Taxis are available throughout Taniti City.

Rental Cars

Rental cars are available from an agency near the airport.

Bicycles

Bikes and helmets are available from several vendors. Helmets are required by law.

Walking

Taniti City is fairly flat and walkable. Merriton Landing is also easy to explore on foot.

Cruise Ship

A small cruise ship docks in Yellow Leaf Bay one night each week.
