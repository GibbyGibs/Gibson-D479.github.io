Explore Taniti

Discover the island's attractions, activities, and entertainment.

Beaches

Enjoy sandy and rocky beaches, including white sandy beaches surrounding Yellow Leaf Bay.

Active Volcano

Explore Taniti's mountainous interior and small active volcano.

Rainforest

Hike through Taniti's tropical rainforest or try zip-lining.

Water Activities

Enjoy snorkeling, chartered fishing tours, and boat tours.

Entertainment

Visit pubs, a microbrewery, dance club, movie theater, arcade, bowling alley, and art galleries.

Sightseeing

Explore Merriton Landing, take a bus tour, or experience a helicopter ride.
